SELECTED RESEARCH PROJECT

Research in Focus

FINANCIAL ECONOMETRICS

Modeling Volatility Dynamics on the Ghana Stock Exchange: A GARCH Approach

This research investigates the volatility dynamics of selected equities listed on the Ghana Stock Exchange using Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity (GARCH) modelling.

The study examines conditional volatility and evaluates alternative approaches to financial market risk estimation.

Particular attention is given to Student-t and normal distribution assumptions in Value at Risk estimation and the empirical evaluation of risk models.

Research methods: GARCH modelling, financial time-series analysis, Value at Risk and backtesting.
